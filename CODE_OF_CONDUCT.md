# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## In practice

**Expected:**

- Being welcoming to people at any level of experience — automation and software
  are two deep fields, and almost nobody is expert in both
- Accepting that a review comment is about the change, not about you
- Saying "I was wrong" without ceremony when you were
- Explaining a decision rather than asserting it

**Not acceptable:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Dismissing a question as beneath answering

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to **alexanderhahn.br@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled privately and in confidence.

Responses are proportionate: usually a private word, sometimes a request to edit
or withdraw a comment, and for serious or repeated problems a ban from the
project. The maintainer decides, and will say plainly what was decided and why.
